## Permanent rules

- **Never request, curl, fetch, open or test Amazon affiliate links** (link.amazon, amzn.to, amazon.com, or any link with `tag=forgealone-20`) in any check, script or browser test. Count and compare them as text only. Clicking them registers fake clicks in Amazon Associates.
