About

Built by someone who ships alone

Forge Alone is written and maintained independently — not by a review team, not by a content agency. Every tool on this site got picked because it came up while building something real, not from a sponsor list.

How a tool gets reviewed

Nothing runs here on the strength of a press kit. Most reviews come from a tool actually being used on a real project long enough to know where it holds up and where it doesn't — which is why every review has a "where it doesn't" section, not just a features list. Occasionally a review starts from the tool's own documentation, pricing pages, and outside user reports instead of hands-on use — when that's the case, the review says so plainly, and gets revisited once hands-on testing catches up. Either way, if a tool isn't worth its cost for a solo builder specifically, that's what the review says, whether or not there's a commission attached to the link.

How this site makes money

Some links on this site are affiliate links, meaning a purchase made through them may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. That relationship never decides which tools get reviewed or what a review concludes — see the full disclosure for specifics.
