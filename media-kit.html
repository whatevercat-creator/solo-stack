Forge Alone, at a glance

For program managers and partners deciding whether Forge Alone is a fit — what the site is, who reads it, and what's available to work with.

The short version

Forge Alone is an independently written, single-author review site covering the tools solo builders actually pay for — security, planning, lead gen, and monetization. Every review has a "where it doesn't hold up" section, not just a features list.

Who reads it

Solo and small-team SaaS founders, indie hackers, and independent builders evaluating whether a tool is worth its cost for someone without a team behind them — the audience that gets underserved by reviews written for enterprise buyers or by content designed purely to rank for a keyword.

What's covered

Current reviews span security (Ledger), planning (Miro), lead gen (involve.me), and monetization infrastructure (Reditus) — see the full list on the homepage. New reviews are added as tools get used on real projects; nothing is reviewed from a press kit alone.

How reviews get written

One person, hands-on: a tool is used on an actual build before it's reviewed, and the review's conclusion doesn't change based on whether a link is monetized. Background on the process is on the About page; how affiliate relationships work is on the disclosure page.

Reach

Forge Alone is a newly launched site — traffic and audience figures will be added here as they become meaningful. Program managers are welcome to ask directly in the meantime.
