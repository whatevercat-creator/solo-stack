Safe bets

  • Things they use daily but wouldn't buy for themselves: a mug warmer, a desk mat, good pens
  • Kits that include everything, so they can start the day they open it
  • Undated or sample-size versions that can't be the wrong year or the wrong scent

Think twice

  • Anything that depends on their exact computer, monitor or phone
  • Large items for a small desk. If in doubt, go smaller
  • A full-size perfume or a dated planner, unless you know their taste

Questions people ask

What is a good gift for someone who works from home?

Something they will use every day but would not buy for themselves, like a mug warmer, a desk mat or good pens. Gifts that give them a reason to step away from the screen, such as a hobby kit, work well too.

What can I buy if I don't know their desk setup?

Choose gifts that don't depend on their computer, monitor or phone, like a desk mat, a cable organizer set or a weekly planner pad. If you are unsure about space, go smaller.

What is a good screen-free gift for someone on a computer all day?

A hands-on hobby kit: air-dry clay, beginner embroidery, a book-nook kit or candle making. Pick a kit that includes everything, so they can start the day they open it.

Is an undated planner a better gift than a dated one?

Usually, yes. An undated planner can't be the wrong year, and there are no wasted pages if they skip a week.

How do I buy perfume as a gift without knowing their taste?

Give a fragrance discovery set: small sizes of several scents, so they can find one they love. Look for a set from one brand with at least five or six samples.
