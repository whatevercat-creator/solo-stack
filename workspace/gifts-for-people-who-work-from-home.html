15 gifts for people who work from home

Buying for a remote worker, a freelancer or someone building a business alone? The best gifts make the workday a little better or give them a reason to step away from the screen. These 15 are practical and affordable, and none of them needs you to know what computer they own.

The short version

Not sure where to start? A desk mug warmer for the coffee drinker, an air-dry clay kit for the person who needs a break from screens, and a dotted journal for the planner. All three are easy to wrap and hard to get wrong.

For the coffee drinker

These three tie in with the desk-side coffee station guide, and each works on its own as a gift.

The gift for anyone whose coffee goes cold during calls. A small warming plate keeps a mug at drinking temperature through a long meeting. Look for: an auto shut-off, and a flat-bottomed mug included if you want it to feel complete.

For the desk

Small upgrades to the place they spend most of the day. Nothing here needs you to know their computer or monitor.

4. A felt or leather desk mat

Makes a desk feel finished, protects the surface, and gives the keyboard and mouse one home. One of the highest-impact desk gifts for the money. Look for: a size that fits a typical desk (around 31 by 15 inches is common) and a neutral colour.

5. A monitor stand with storage

Raises the screen to eye level and clears space underneath for a notebook or keyboard. Good for anyone working from a laptop or a small desk. Look for: solid wood or bamboo, and a drawer or shelf underneath.

6. A cable organizer set

Not glamorous, but people rarely buy it for themselves and are glad to have it. A box for the power strip plus a few cable clips tidies a whole desk. Look for: a ventilated box and adhesive clips in more than one size.

For time away from the screen

Hands-on hobbies are one of the biggest gift themes this year, and they suit anyone who looks at a screen all day.

7. An air-dry clay kit

No kiln, no experience needed. Trinket dishes and bag charms are popular first projects. It's an evening away from the laptop in a box. Look for: a kit with tools and paint included, so they can start the day they open it.

8. A beginner embroidery kit

Quiet, portable and satisfying. A good starter kit comes with the pattern printed on the fabric, so there's no guessing. Look for: a pre-printed pattern, a hoop, and all the thread and needles included.

Paper planning tools for people who are their own boss.

Safe bets

  • Things they use daily but wouldn't buy for themselves: a mug warmer, a desk mat, good pens
  • Kits that include everything, so they can start the day they open it
  • Undated or sample-size versions that can't be the wrong year or the wrong scent

Think twice

  • Anything that depends on their exact computer, monitor or phone
  • Large items for a small desk. If in doubt, go smaller
  • A full-size perfume or a dated planner, unless you know their taste

The café-at-home set: a milk frother, syrup pump bottles and a double-wall mug. The desk reset: a desk mat, a cable organizer set and a weekly planner pad. The screen-free evening: a clay or embroidery kit and a candle-making kit.
