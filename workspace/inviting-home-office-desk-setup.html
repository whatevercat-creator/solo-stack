Make your home office inviting: 12 desk setup upgrades for work and play

Making your home office inviting is what it's all about. You'll be spending untold hours in that space, so why not make it enjoyable? These 12 upgrades work whether the desk is for client calls, late-night building or a few rounds of a game once the work is done.

The short version

Start with light, then surface, then tidy: a strip of LED lighting behind the desk, a large desk mat, and a cable management kit. Those three change how the space feels more than anything else here, and none of them depends on what computer you own.

Light it well

Lighting changes how a desk feels more than anything else on this list, and it is the heart of the glowing setups all over Pinterest right now.

1. LED strip lights behind the desk

A strip along the back of the desk or monitor throws a soft glow on the wall. It makes the room feel finished in the evening and takes the harsh edge off a bright screen in a dark room. Look for: a strip long enough for your desk, a remote or app for colour and brightness, and adhesive backing.

2. A monitor light bar

Sits on top of the screen and lights the desk without glare on the display or a lamp taking up space. One of the tidiest lighting upgrades for a small desk. Look for: adjustable brightness and colour temperature, and a clip that fits your monitor's thickness.

Browse monitor light bars on Amazon →

3. A warm, dimmable desk lamp

Overhead lights make a workspace feel like an office. A small lamp with a warm bulb makes it feel like a room you chose to be in. Look for: a dimmer and a warm setting. Keep it small if your desk is.

Browse desk lamps on Amazon →

Make the surface nice to use

The things your hands touch all day.

4. A large desk mat

Ties the keyboard and mouse together, protects the surface and softens the look of the whole desk. The same pick as in the gift guide, because it works that well. Look for: a size that covers keyboard and mouse together, and a colour that suits the room.

Browse desk mats on Amazon →

5. A monitor arm

Lifts the screen off the desk, frees the space underneath and lets you set the height and distance to suit you. It is also what gives those clean setups their floating-screen look. Look for: check your monitor's weight and that it has VESA mounting holes on the back before you buy.

Browse monitor arms on Amazon →

6. A keyboard wrist rest

A small strip of cushioning in front of the keyboard. Cheap, and you notice it from the first hour. Look for: one that matches your keyboard's width, with a non-slip base.

Browse wrist rests on Amazon →

Be comfortable for the long haul

If you are going to sit there for hours, small comforts add up.

7. An under-desk footrest

Gives your feet somewhere to be other than dangling or tucked under the chair. Especially good if your desk or chair is a little too high. Look for: an adjustable angle and a non-slip surface.

Browse footrests on Amazon →

8. A lumbar cushion

Makes an ordinary chair more pleasant to sit in without buying a new one. Look for: a strap to hold it in place and a washable cover.

9. A headphone stand

Gives headphones a home instead of the corner of the desk, and looks good doing it. Some include extra USB ports. Look for: a weighted base so it doesn't tip. A hook that clamps under the desk is the space-saving alternative.

Browse headphone stands on Amazon →

Keep it tidy and make it yours

A desk you enjoy is one that is clear of clutter and has a few things on show that you like.

10. A cable management kit

Nothing ruins a nice setup faster than a nest of cables. A box for the power strip, a tray under the desk and a handful of clips sort it out in an afternoon. Look for: an under-desk tray if you have a monitor arm or standing desk, and clips in more than one size.

Browse cable management kits on Amazon →

11. A pegboard for the wall

Gets headphones, controllers, cables and small tools off the desk and onto the wall, where they become part of the look. Look for: a kit that includes hooks, shelves and mounting hardware.

Browse pegboards on Amazon →

12. A small display shelf

For the plant, the figures, the things you collect. Trinket shelves are having a moment, and a desk wall is a good place for one. Look for: floating shelves sized to the wall above your desk, with hardware included.

Browse display shelves on Amazon →

Worth it if

  • You spend most of your working day, and some of your evenings, at the same desk
  • Your workspace still feels like a spare corner rather than a room you chose
  • You want one setup that suits both focused work and time off

Skip or wait if

  • You are about to move or change desks. Measure the new space first
  • Your monitor has no mounting holes on the back. Skip the arm and use a stand
  • You are tempted to buy all twelve at once. Start with light, surface and tidy
