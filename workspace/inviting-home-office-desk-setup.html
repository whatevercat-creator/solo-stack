Light it well

Lighting changes how a desk feels more than anything else on this list, and it is the heart of the glowing setups all over Pinterest right now.

1. LED strip lights behind the desk

A strip along the back of the desk or monitor throws a soft glow on the wall. It makes the room feel finished in the evening and takes the harsh edge off a bright screen in a dark room. Look for: a strip long enough for your desk, a remote or app for colour and brightness, and adhesive backing.

Worth it if

  • You spend most of your working day, and some of your evenings, at the same desk
  • Your workspace still feels like a spare corner rather than a room you chose
  • You want one setup that suits both focused work and time off

Skip or wait if

  • You are about to move or change desks. Measure the new space first
  • Your monitor has no mounting holes on the back. Skip the arm and use a stand
  • You are tempted to buy all twelve at once. Start with light, surface and tidy

Questions people ask

What should I upgrade first to make my home office inviting?

Start with light, then the surface, then tidying: an LED strip behind the desk, a large desk mat and a cable management kit. Those three change how the space feels the most, and none of them depends on what computer you own.

What size desk mat do I need for a small desk?

Pick one big enough to sit under your keyboard and mouse together, so both share one surface. Measure your desk first and leave a little room at the edges so the mat lies flat instead of hanging over.

Where do LED strip lights go on a desk?

Along the back of the desk or the back of the monitor, so the light washes the wall instead of shining at you. That glow behind the screen takes the harsh edge off a bright display in a dark room. Get a strip long enough for the run, with adhesive backing.

Will a monitor arm fit my monitor?

Check two things before you buy: that your monitor has VESA mounting holes on the back, and that its weight is within what the arm supports. If there are no mounting holes, skip the arm and use a monitor stand instead.

Is a keyboard wrist rest worth it?

It is a small upgrade you notice from the first hour. Choose one that matches your keyboard's width and has a non-slip base so it stays put. The wrist rest set in this guide is the one pick here that I own and use.
