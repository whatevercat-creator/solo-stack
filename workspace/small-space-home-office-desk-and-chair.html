Small-space home office: the desk, the chair and the comfort extras

Most home offices are a corner of another room. That makes two decisions matter more than any gadget: a desk that fits the space you have, and a seat you can stay in for a full working day. This guide covers both, plus the cushions and footrest that make an ordinary chair far more comfortable.

The short version

Measure first, then spend on the chair before the desk. If a new chair isn't in the budget, a seat cushion, a lumbar cushion and a footrest will improve the one you have for a fraction of the cost.

The desk

Measure the wall or corner first, then pick the shape that fits it. In a small room the desk decides where everything else goes.

1. A compact writing desk

The simplest answer for a spare corner or a bedroom wall: a plain top around 40 inches wide, sometimes with one drawer. Big enough for a laptop, a lamp and a notebook, and small enough to leave the room usable. Look for: a width that fits your wall with a few inches spare, a depth of at least 20 inches, and a crossbar that won't hit your knees.

The chair

The chair matters more than the desk. You notice a bad desk when you look at it and a bad chair all day.

4. An ergonomic mesh office chair

The safe default: a breathable mesh back, adjustable height and built-in lumbar support. You do not need the most expensive one, but adjustability is what separates a chair you can sit in for a day from one you can't. Look for: adjustable seat height and lumbar support, armrests that move or flip up, and a weight rating that covers you.

Before you buy

  • Measure the wall, the corner and the doorway the box has to come through
  • Check the desk height against your chair: most desks are 28 to 30 inches high
  • Read the return terms. Desks and chairs are bulky to send back

Think twice

  • A desk under 20 inches deep, unless it only ever holds a laptop
  • A chair with no height adjustment, however good it looks
  • Buying desk and chair together as a matched set. Choose each on its own merits

Questions people ask

What size desk fits a small home office?

A compact desk around 40 inches wide suits most spare corners and bedroom walls, with room for a laptop, a lamp and a notebook. Aim for a depth of at least 20 inches, and measure the wall so you have a few inches spare.

How tall should a desk be?

Most desks are 28 to 30 inches high, so check that against your chair before you buy. If the desk ends up a little high for you, a footrest gives your feet a flat place to rest.

What kind of chair fits under a small desk?

An armless chair, or one with flip-up arms, because armrests are usually what stop a chair tucking fully under the desk. Make sure the seat still adjusts for height and the wheels suit your floor.

Do I need a chair mat on carpet?

On carpet, a mat lets the chair roll instead of digging in, and it protects the floor. Carpet mats have grippers underneath while hard-floor mats are smooth, so buy the type for your floor and size it to cover your full rolling range.

How can I make my desk chair more comfortable without buying a new one?

A lumbar cushion fills the gap behind your lower back that most ordinary chairs leave empty, and a seat cushion takes pressure off on long days. If your feet don't rest flat on the floor, add a footrest.
